Past Research

The lab builds on Sai Tej Paruchuri’s earlier research at Lehigh University and Virginia Tech. This work spans fusion plasma control, kernel methods for estimation and learning, and vibration attenuation in smart structures.

Fusion plasma control

Plasma Control Laboratory, Lehigh University

Tokamaks are the leading approach to magnetic-confinement fusion energy. Operating them safely requires feedback control of coupled, nonlinear plasma dynamics with a limited set of shared actuators. This work developed model-based and learning-based controllers for plasma density, the safety-factor and temperature profiles, and burn conditions. It addressed disruption avoidance, actuator constraints, and the allocation of shared actuators across competing objectives. Control methods from this work were evaluated experimentally on tokamaks such as DIII-D (USA), KSTAR (South Korea), and TCV (Switzerland). Control design studies also targeted NSTX-U, EAST, and ITER.

Selected publications

  • Density regulation with disruption avoidance in next-generation tokamaks using a safe reinforcement learning-based controller, Fusion Engineering and Design, 2025
  • Static actuator-sharing algorithm for concurrent control of multiple plasma properties, Plasma Physics and Controlled Fusion, 2024
  • Nonlinear control of the minimum safety factor in tokamaks by optimal allocation of spatially moving electron cyclotron current drive, Fusion Engineering and Design, 2024

Kernel methods, Koopman operators, and learning

With collaborators at Virginia Tech

Reproducing kernel Hilbert space (RKHS) methods give a rigorous framework for learning unknown nonlinear functions from data while they are being used for estimation and control. This work studied adaptive estimation in RKHS, including sufficient conditions for parameter convergence and persistence of excitation over embedded manifolds. It also covered approximation of Koopman operators, and convergence rates for reinforcement learning and online critic approximation in native spaces.

Selected publications

  • Sufficient conditions for parameter convergence over embedded manifolds using kernel techniques, IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control, 2023
  • Approximation of discrete and orbital Koopman operators over subsets and manifolds, Nonlinear Dynamics, 2024
  • Kernel center adaptation in the reproducing kernel Hilbert space embedding method, International Journal of Adaptive Control and Signal Processing, 2022

Smart structures and vibration attenuation

Virginia Tech

Subordinate oscillator arrays are sets of small resonators attached to a primary structure, and they can provide broadband vibration attenuation. This work developed passive piezoelectric subordinate oscillator arrays, studied how errors in the distribution of resonator properties affect performance and how to recover it, and used RKHS embedding methods for adaptive estimation of nonlinearities in piezoelectric systems.

Selected publications

  • Passive piezoelectric subordinate oscillator arrays, Smart Materials and Structures, 2019
  • Reproducing kernel Hilbert space embedding for adaptive estimation of nonlinearities in piezoelectric systems, Nonlinear Dynamics, 2020
  • Expanding the teaching of single frequency vibration absorption to broadband attenuation using subordinate oscillator arrays via fettuccine pasta, Engineering Structures, 2020
